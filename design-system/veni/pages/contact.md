# Contact Page Overrides

> **PROJECT:** Veni
> **Page Type:** Content / Support

> Rules here **override** `design-system/veni/MASTER.md`.

---

## Page-Specific Rules

### Layout Overrides

- **Max width:** `40rem` centred (`.content-page`) — long-form measure, not full catalog width.
- **Sections:** Header (title + one lead sentence) > Contact facts (email, hours, response) > Short cross-links to Orders and Shipping & returns.
- **No form.** Contact is mailto + facts until a real inbox workflow exists. Do not invent a fake submit button.

### Typography Overrides

- Page title uses heading Jost (not Bodoni). Bodoni stays on the homepage hero only.
- Fact labels are uppercase tracked chrome; values are sentence case.

### Color Overrides

- No accent gold. Borders and muted labels only.

### Component Overrides

- Reuse `.page-header`, `.section__title`, and `.content-page__*` — do not introduce cards or icon rows.
